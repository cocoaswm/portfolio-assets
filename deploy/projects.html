02

Support Operations Portal

Overview: An internal portal for IT support operations, used by all 7 members of the support team in their daily work.

Verification: About 19 automated test specs (Vitest unit tests and Playwright end-to-end tests), alongside hands-on functional testing against real shift routines. Designed around ISO 27001-style data-handling constraints — a self-imposed design rule, not a certification.

Technologies used:Next.jsTypeScriptTailwind CSSZustandJWTVitestPlaywrightDockernginx
DEMO ACCESS

Demo account. Read-only access.

Open Live Demo
What the app can do:
  • SLA dashboard: open cases, cases close to breaching (under 30 min), breached and unassigned cases, with auto refresh and quick case logging.
  • Ticket queue filterable by status and agent; Freshdesk tickets can arrive through an optional inbound webhook, and the portal makes no outbound calls to third-party APIs.
  • Shift handover that summarises open and pending cases (office, day, night), which the next shift must acknowledge.
  • Knowledge hub with tagged knowledge cards, fuzzy search, manuals and a reply-script library.
  • On-call schedule with roster import, shift-duty KPIs, an audit log and page-level access permissions.
  • Attendance, monitoring alerts, daily and weekly reports, and an audit history.

04

PukPui Pay - Debt Tracker with a Thai Bank Interest Engine

Overview: An installable web app for two users to track shared debts, with an interest engine built around Thai bank rules. Live in production on a VPS; the public demo uses synthetic data only, is wiped every night, and is fully isolated from real financial data.

Verification: Each lender’s rules were derived from real statements and checked by an automated backtest suite of 80+ checks across 7 products from Thai lenders. Calculated interest matches the printed bills to within about 2 THB per cycle, and most checks to within 0.05 THB.

Technologies used:Next.jsTypeScriptPrismaPostgreSQLPWADocker Compose
DEMO ACCESS

Demo environment with synthetic data only. Sign in with the demo PIN.

Open Live Demo
What the app can do:
  • Monthly view: total due this month, each person’s share, payment progress, and marking items as paid.
  • Filters by person and type (car, card, loans, monthly bills) and sorting by remaining balance.
  • Debt overview: total debt, percentage paid, and installments paid / remaining for each debt.
  • Credit card bills & interest: statement-cycle interest that follows Thai bank rules, a principal and interest split, next-bill forecasts and the minimum payment due.
  • Payoff forecast: enter a monthly amount to see how long it takes to clear the card, total interest and a month-by-month schedule, and compare paying the minimum with paying in full.
  • Due-date notifications, installable as a PWA, with a tablet-friendly layout.
